The Real Problems with Sending Money to India From the USA

The trouble starts with cost. Money sent abroad rarely arrives whole, because fees and the gap between exchange rates quietly reduce what lands on the other side.

This is not a small corridor. Many of these transfers are personal, sent to support parents, children, and households back home.

The cost of moving that money stays stubbornly high. The World Bank reports that sending money internationally cost an average of about 6.4% of the amount near the end of 2023, more than double the 3% target set under the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, though transfers sent digitally tend to cost less. On top of cost, older methods can be slow and hard to track, so you are often left wondering whether the money made it.
